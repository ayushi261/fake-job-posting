1. System Execution & Data Flow

End-to-end architecture transforming raw 18-attribute inputs into real-time risk predictions.

Step 01

Drive Data Ingestion

Mounts Google Drive and reads 17,880 observations across all 18 raw attributes.

Step 02

Feature Engineering

Drops job_id, merges 5 text fields into full_text, extracts flags (has_salary).

Step 03

NLP & Vectorization

RegEx cleaning, lemmatization, and 3,000 unigram/bigram TF-IDF vector transformation.

Step 04

Stratified Train/Test

80/20 train-test split preserving identical 95:5 class imbalance ratios across subsets.

Step 05

Weighted Inference

Model evaluation prioritizing Recall to minimize False Negatives in live risk scores.
