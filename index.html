How it was built

01

Clean & explore

Loaded EMSCAD dataset, merged unstructured text fields, checked nulls, and built metadata correlation matrix.

02

Engineer & vectorize

Extracted 3,000 TF-IDF unigram/bigrams, engineered binary indicator flags, and applied stratified splitting.

03

Train & compare

Three classifiers fit on stratified split and evaluated for maximum recall to prevent uncaught job fraud.
